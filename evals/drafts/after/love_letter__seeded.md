The house is so very quiet when I come back in the evenings. I keep reaching for two mugs. I am counting the days now, and I will admit to being an impatient lover: the long days and hours wing their slow flight by.

My day used to start with your smile every morning. Now it starts with your voice on the phone, which is good but not the same. I love you more and more every day, and I mean that as a plain report, not a line. Come home soon.
